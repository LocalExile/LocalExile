# Act 3 quests

Act 3 is the city of Sarn. The player arrives from Act 2's The Ancient Pyramid in The City of Sarn (`1_3_1`) and the
town is The Sarn Encampment (`1_3_town`, NPCs Clarissa, Grigor, Hargan, Maramoa). The main line is a chain of gates:
rescue Clarissa and get Tolman's bracelet (a3q1, Lost in Love) so Clarissa gives the Sewer Keys (a3q2); the keys open
The Sewers and with it the whole middle of the city (`a3q11-UsedSewerKeys` gates seven areas); find Lady Dialla in
The Solaris Temple Level 2 (a3q3), return her Ribbon Spool (a3q4) and Thaumetic Sulphite and burn the Undying
blockage in The Sewers with her Infernal Talc (a3q5; `a3q5-UsedTalc` gates the Ebony Barracks and everything west of
the river); kill Piety in The Lunaris Temple Level 2 for the Tower Key (a3q9); unlock the Sceptre of God and kill
Dominus atop The Upper Sceptre of God (a3q10). Lady Dialla then appears on the tower and the exit leads to Act 4's
The Aqueduct (`1_4_1`). Side quests: Sever the Right Hand (a3q8, General Gravicius), Victario's Secrets (a3q11, three
busts, passive point + 2 respec points), A Fixture of Fate (a3q12, Siosa's Golden Pages), A Swig of Hope (a3q13,
Fairgraves, only if he was killed in Act 1). Two Books of Skill: a3q11 and a3q9.
Sources: client tables (`data/tables`), the generated layouts (`lg.ps1 questobjects`, seed 1), room files
(`lg.ps1 cat`), unpacked `.ot`/`.it` text, the wiki (Fandom mirror, 2026-10-30), poedb not needed.

## Status (implemented, played live 2026-10-30)

Every quest of the act runs on the engine (`Modules/Core/World/QuestObjects/Acts/Act3.cs`,
[12](../../protocol/12-quests.md) §12) and was played unattended by the campaign runner
(`scripts/dev/campaign/quests/a3q*.py`, `a2q8a3.py`) through to Highgate, with every flag of `Act3Complete`, before the
game moved into modules; on the modules the oracle (`QuestEngineTests`) plays it after Acts 1 and 2 to `Act3Complete`.
Clarissa is freed by her guards' captain's death; Tolman's chair, Victario's stashes, the book stands (Chests.dat rows
without an `.ot`) and the other quest chests open; the Sewer Keys and the Infernal Talc are built as rewards and used on
the grating and the BioWall; Hargan's hand-in sets `a3q11-DeliveredBust1`–`3`; Fairgraves stands in The Docks after his
Act 1 death; offer 21 builds a magic amulet (no fallback needed); the Library's candle opens the hidden door for the
instance (an engine instance state); Dominus dies into his second form and Lady Dialla appears at the
DeathPortalLocator.

The tower top's exit to The Aqueduct (city_tower.arm's `entrance2disabled`, which the layout disables) is created
because a3q10 gates it (`AreaTransitionCatalog.Gated`), and shown once `a3q10-KilledDominus` is set. The Library's way
into The Archives (exit_1.arm's `entrance2disabled`) is created the same way, gated by the candle's instance state. The
offline exit audit ([08](../../protocol/08-area-transitions.md) §8) lists both as `gated` at seed 1 and its three other
seeds; without `--quests` (no quest tables) both are dropped as arrival points:

```powershell
dotnet run --project scripts/re/exits -c Release -- "^1_3_1[78]" --quests
```

The sections after this one are the research done before the act was built: their "Risks" are what was expected then.

  client blockers `EpicDoorBlocker`. `FlagToEnter(1_3_15 -> 1_3_18_1)` = `a3q9-UsedTowerKey`.
- `a3q10-KilledDominus`: EndQuestFlags of 1_4_town Highgate, 1_4_1 The Aqueduct, 1_4_2 The Dried Lake.
  `FlagToEnter(1_3_18_2 -> 1_4_1)` = `a3q10-KilledDominus`. QS 235 places the unlock after `a4q1-QuestReceived`
  (Dialla's 707); the client gate only needs the kill, so keep the server check on the kill.

### Act 3 -> Act 4 transition
The player is moved by an ordinary area transition: the tower top's exit (tile record
`Act3_tower_01_01_area_transition2`, type `Metadata/MiscellaneousObjects/AreaTransitionMapMarker`) leads from
`1_3_18_2` The Upper Sceptre of God to `1_4_1` The Aqueduct (WorldAreas connection), which sets `Visited-4_1`;
Highgate (`1_4_town`, `Visited-4_town`) is next. No NPC script moves the player. Flags at that point:
`a3q10-KilledDominus`, `a3q8-DiallaCongratulated`, `a4q1-QuestReceived` (if Dialla was talked to on the tower).

### Risks
- Dominus is the biggest Act 3 blocker: no room point for the boss (scripted spawn), a two-form fight (Pope
  `set_cannot_die` -> Dominusdemon), no table setter for `a3q10-DominusFightStarted`, `-SeenDominus`,
  `-KilledDominus`, `-SeenDialla`. A minimal version: spawn Pope on entering the tower top, replace it with
  Dominusdemon when its life reaches 0 (`/kill` must take that path), set the flag on Dominusdemon's death, spawn
  DiallaTower (high).
- Which variety's death sets `a3q10-KilledDominus` is inferred (1678 cannot die); not verified in client code.
- The tower exit: whether the Gateway creates the `AreaTransitionMapMarker` record as a usable transition today, and
  whether BossArenaEntranceTransition is an in-area teleport, are unverified (shared with Act 2's pyramid).
- Scion: NPCTalk 705 (`Metadata/NPC/Act3/Scion`, "Unlocked", QF4 `ScionUnlocked`, Script `triggerer.EnableScion();
  triggerer.FinishNPCChat();`) and `Metadata/NPC/Act3/ScionCage` belong to Act 3 but no generated room or tile places
  them; not a quest row, not needed by the campaign (it unlocks the Scion class for the account). Location unverified.
