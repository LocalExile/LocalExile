# Act 8 quests

Act 8 starts in The Sarn Ramparts (2_8_1, entered from The Temple of Decay Level 2 once `a7q4-KilledBoss` is set) and
its town is The Sarn Encampment (2_8_town: Hargan, Maramoa, Clarissa, Sin; Tolman stands there without dialogue).
The main line is `a8q1` "Essence of the Hag" (Sin gives it, Doedre dies in Doedre's Cesspool): `a8q1-KilledBoss`
is the EndQuestFlags gate of every later act 8 area (2_8_3 … 2_8_14). Then `a8q2` "Lunar Eclipse" and `a8q3`
"Solar Eclipse" run together (Maramoa): Dusk drops the Moon Orb in The Lunaris Temple Level 2, Dawn the Sun Orb in
The Solaris Temple Level 2, and both orbs summon Solaris and Lunaris on The Harbour Bridge. Their death sets
`a8q23-KilledBosses`, the gate of every act 9 area; the act ends by walking from the bridge into The Blood Aqueduct
(2_9_1). Side quests: `a8q4` Reflection of Terror (Yugul, book), `a8q5` The Wings of Vastiri (Hector Titucius'
tomb), `a8q6` Love is Dead (Ankh of Eternity, Clarissa at the Quay, book), `a8q7` The Gemling Legion (book).
Act 8's Pantheon flags: `a8q23-KilledBosses` (major gods Solaris and Lunaris) and `a8q4-KilledBoss` (minor god Yugul).
Sources: client tables (`data/tables`, 3.29.3b), the generated layouts (`lg.ps1 questobjects`, seed 1, and `lg.ps1
grep/cat` of the rooms), the unpacked game text (`.ot`/`.it`), the wiki (Fandom mirror, 2026-10-30), poedb.

## Status

On the quest engine (`Modules/Core/World/QuestObjects/Acts/Act8.cs`, [12](../../protocol/12-quests.md) §17), with
driver steps `a8q1`, `a8q5`, `a8q4`, `a8q6`, `a8q7`, `a8q3` (both eclipses' fight), `a8q2` (the arrival in Act 9). Played
live on 2026-10-31 before the game moved into modules; on the modules the oracle (`QuestEngineTests`, to
`E3EndOfAct8`'s act 7–8 flags) and the offline exit audit check it, not yet the client. The sections after the status
are the research done before the act was built. What differs from the
sections below:
- Every boss is placed (the numbered boss points: Dusk at `mapboss1`, the legion at `mapboss1–7`, Tolman at his spawner,
  Solaris and Lunaris creatable). Dusk and Dawn drop the orbs (the orb objects' spawners are in no room).
- The fight starter takes both orbs (`a8q23-DeliveredItems`) and brings Solaris and Lunaris where it stands (the
  population leaves them out until then); `On.KilledAll` sets `a8q23-KilledBosses` once both are dead. The bridge's exit
  into The Blood Aqueduct (a transition its tile disables) is created and gated by that flag.
- The legion: `a8q7-KilledBoss` once none of the seven is alive (`On.KilledAll`).
- a8q6: Clarissa at the Quay (`ClarissaQuay`, Transitionable with `a8q6-DeliveredItem`) takes the Ankh with NPCTalk 1153,
  which in 3.29 also sets `a8q6-KilledBoss`; Tolman stands at his spawner but need not die.
- Not created: the statue orbs and breaks, the arena doors.

### Risks
- **Harbour Bridge fight (high):** Lunaris/Solaris are not spawnable (Timer component), their room `bridge_arena.arm`
  has only `mapboss1`/`mapboss2` (not in `room-points.json`), the fight starter is a room server object not created,
  and the fight is scripted (statue → fight → transform → super form when one dies). Minimum: create
  `GoddessFightStarter`, on use with both orbs consume them, set `a8q23-DeliveredItems`, spawn both goddesses at the
  two points in fight state (Transitionable 2), set `a8q23-KilledBosses` when both are dead.
- **Dusk not placed:** `pietyfight_2_1.arm` names its point `mapboss1`. Generalise the boss placement to
  `mapboss<N>` / `force:mapboss` (one shared prerequisite: "numbered mapboss points"; same fix as the Gemling
  Legion, Shakari, the Trinity, Kitava). A seed-1 layout run of 2_8_7_2 also logged "Could not find matching overlap
  tile at (140, 28)": check that the Gateway generates The Lunaris Temple Level 2 with its arena room.
- **Orb source unverified:** the wiki says Dusk/Dawn drop the orbs; the client has orb objects (`LunarisOrb`,
  `SolarisOrb`) with spawners that appear in no room or tile. Dropping the item on the kill is the simplest server
  behaviour and gives the same flags.
- `a8q2-SeenMiniboss`/`KilledMiniboss`, `a8q3-…`, `a8q23-DeliveredItems`, `a8q23-KilledBosses`: no client setter, all
  server script.
