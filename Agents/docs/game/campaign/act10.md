# Act 10 quests

Act 10 starts in Oriath Docks (2_10_town, gate `a9q1-KilledBoss`), reached from The Rotting Core by Lilly's NPC
script (`AreaTransitionTo(2_10_town)`). Town NPCs: Lani, Bannon (later Innocence), Sin, Weylam Roth, Lilly Roth.
The main line: `a10q1` "Safe Passage" (rescue Bannon on The Cathedral Rooftop from the cultists and Plaguewing),
`a10q2` "Death and Rebirth" (Avarius' Staff of Purity from The Desecrated Chambers; Bannon becomes Innocence) and
`a10q3` "An End to Hunger" (Innocence breaks Kitava's Horns in The Ravaged Square, `a10q3-OpenedGate` opens The
Canals and The Feeding Trough; Sin starts the final fight; Kitava's death sets `EndGameAvailable`, which applies the
second −30% resistance penalty and opens the Epilogue). Then `A11q0` "Toward the Future": Lilly's "Set Sail from
Oriath" sets `A10LillySailedAway` and moves the player to Karui Shores (2_11_endgame_town). `a10q3a11` is the
Epilogue (act 11) copy of a10q3's last steps (Lani's book in Karui Shores). Side quests: `a10q4` No Love for Old
Ghosts (Elixir of Allure, Ossuary, Book of Regrets), `a10q5` Map to Tsoatha (The Teardrop, Reliquary, rare belt),
`a10q6` Vilenta's Vengeance (Control Blocks, book). No Pantheon flag comes from act 10 quests.
Sources: client tables (`data/tables`, 3.29.3b), the generated layouts (`lg.ps1 questobjects`, seed 1, and `lg.ps1
grep/cat` of the rooms), the unpacked game text (`.ot`/`.it`), the wiki (Fandom mirror, 2026-10-30), poedb. The
sections after the status are the research done before the act was built.

## Status

On the quest engine (`Modules/Core/World/QuestObjects/Acts/Act10.cs`, [12](../../protocol/12-quests.md) §19; the
Epilogue's quests need no rules, so it has no act file), with driver steps `a10q1`, `a10q6`, `a10q4`, `a10q5`, `a10q2`,
`a10q3`, `A11q0`, `a10q3a11`. Played live on 2026-10-31 before the game moved into modules; on the modules the oracle
(`QuestEngineTests`: every quest of acts 7–10 and the Epilogue completes, `EpilogueAtlasDemo`'s act 7–10 flags, the
campaign's 24 quest passive and 20 refund points, Kitava's penalty −30% before him and −60% after, Karui Shores) and
the offline exit audit check it, not yet the client. What differs from the sections below:
- a10q1: coming near Bannon brings Plaguewing from its spawner (`On.SpawnsNear`; the cultist waves are not run); its
  death sets `a10q1-RescuedBannon` and Bannon stands (Transitionable 2).
- a10q2: Bannon (`BannonTown`) goes and Innocence (`InnocenceTown`, state 2) stands on the same cell with
  `a10q2-BannonTransformed`.
- a10q3: the horn gate into The Canals is created from the layout's graph node (so is the way to The Torched Courts,
  `OriathSquareToCourts`) and gated by `a10q3-OpenedGate`; the horn blockage transitions and its twelve
  blockers lift. Kitava (`set_cannot_die`) never dies: the hit that would kill him, or `/kill`, ends the fight
  (`On.Threshold`), sets `EndGameAvailable` and he leaves. Every character is given `EndGameAvailable` on login for the
  Atlas; Kitava's sets it as earned (saved), and only then does the Act 10 penalty count (`ResistancePenalty`: −30%
  elemental and chaos, −60% with Act 5's) and `/state` list the flag.
- Sin's portal back to the docks (`EpiloguePortal`) opens with `EndGameAvailable` (the post-fight Sin who opens it, a
  monster with an NPC component, is not created). Played live 2026-10-31 (a10q1-a10q6, A11q0, no `/goto`); the
  Pearl Case on its pedestal is used from the nearest floor (`PlayerWalk.InReach`); InnocenceTown stands on Bannon's
  cell (TownNpcs).
- Burnt Oriath has Act 5's tiles: an exit Act 5's rules give a destination of its own (`On.Exists(…, to:)`: the
  Chamber of Innocence's `InnocenceToDestroyedCourts` to Act 5's Torched Courts) keeps it only in Act 5
  (`AreaTransitionCatalog.SameAct`), so The Desecrated Chambers' same tile leads to Act 10's Torched Courts (2_10_3).
  Act 5's serialisation flags (`On.Exists`) hold here too and match: a character in Act 10 has `a5q4-KilledBoss`, so
  the burnt exit stands and the unburnt `InnocenceToCourts` is gone.
- Before Kitava the Epilogue is shut: `EndGameAvailable`, which every character is given for the Atlas, is sent only in
  a hideout and in the maps until earned (`Quests.EndgameOnly`), so Lilly's "Set Sail from Oriath" (1433), Lani's
  "Kitava" / "Kitava Reward" (1415/1416) and Karui Shores wait for him (the oracle checks all of them).
- Not created: the fight's phases (the heart, the feast bosses). `SinOnKitavaDead` is not set
  (no later quest reads it).
