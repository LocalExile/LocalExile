# Act 9 quests

Act 9 starts in The Blood Aqueduct (2_9_1, entered from The Harbour Bridge once `a8q23-KilledBosses` is set) and its
town is Highgate (2_9_town: Petarus and Vanja, Irasha, Tasuni, Sin). The main line is `a9q1` "Recurring Nightmare":
Sin needs Trarthan Powder (from the Theurgic Precipitate Machine in The Refinery, after General Adus) and Basilisk Acid
(dropped by The Basilisk in The Boiling Lake); at The Quarry he burns a way into The Belly of the Beast
(`SinEnterBeast`, the gate of 2_9_10_1 and 2_9_10_2); in The Rotting Core the spirits of Doedre, Maligaro and
Shavronne die, Sin manifests The Depraved Trinity, its death sets `a9q1-KilledBoss` (the gate of every act 10 area),
and Lilly Roth sails the player to Oriath Docks (NPC script `AreaTransitionTo(2_10_town)`). Side quests: `a9q2` The
Ruler of Highgate (Kira and Garukhan in The Quarry, the Sekhema Feather, book), `a9q3` The Storm Blade (mummy event
chest in The Vastiri Desert, rare weapon), `a9q4` Fastis Fortuna (Boulderback, Book of Regrets), `a9q5` Queen of the
Sands (Bottled Storm, Shakari in The Oasis, book). Act 9's Pantheon flags: `a9q2-KilledBoss` (Garukhan) and
`a9q5-KilledBoss` (Shakari).
Sources: client tables (`data/tables`, 3.29.3b), the generated layouts (`lg.ps1 questobjects`, seed 1, and `lg.ps1
grep/cat` of the rooms), the unpacked game text (`.ot`/`.it`), the wiki (Fandom mirror, 2026-10-30), poedb.

## Status

On the quest engine (`Modules/Core/World/QuestObjects/Acts/Act9.cs`, [12](../../protocol/12-quests.md) §18), with
driver steps `a9q3`, `a9q5`, `a9q4`, `a9q2`, `a9q1`. Played live on 2026-10-31 before the game moved into modules; on
the modules the oracle (`QuestEngineTests`) and the offline exit audit check it, not yet the client. The sections
after the status are the research done before the act was built. What differs from the sections below:
- Every boss is placed (the numbered boss points: Shakari's two stages, the three spirits and the Trinity at their
  numbered points). The Basilisk drops its acid, Garukhan the Sekhema Feather, Boulderback the calendar (no table names
  the droppers: the wiki and the log).
- a9q2: `a9q2-QuestReceived` comes with Tasuni's / Irasha's "Oyun" (1301/1312) and `IrashaTakeFeather` /
  `TasuniTakeFeather` with their "Feather of Sekhema" (1313/1302): `On.TalkRow` (no column sets them). The quest log's
  Irasha ending is Order 1 (Order 0 is Tasuni's); `QuestStates.Complete` takes either.
- a9q3: the mummy event chest is a server object with a StateMachine the quest-object blob does not write: coming to it
  begins the encounter and brings one Sand Warrior and one Sand Goliath (`On.SpawnsNear`); both dead end it and drop the
  Storm Blade.
- a9q5: the Bottled Storm is used by carrying it to the sand barrier (`On.NearWith`: it has no use action and the barrier
  no interaction), which takes it, transitions the barrier and opens The Oasis.
- The membrane into The Belly of the Beast is created from the layout's graph node and gated by `SinEnterBeast`; its seven
  blockers lift with it. SinQuarry, SinCore and Lilly (after the Trinity) are created; the heart portals and the seed of
  corruption are not (the driver teleports to the spirits and the Trinity).

### Risks
- **Depraved Trinity (high):** four bosses on `mapboss1…4` points (none placed today), three out-of-area NPC/portal
  objects (SinCore, sub-boss portals, seed of corruption) and NPC scripts with `ModifyState` and session flags. Minimum:
  place the three spirits, SinCore with rows 1386/1388, the Trinity (targetable state) and Lilly with her transition.
- **Session flags:** `SetSessionQuestFlag` marks `a9q1-PortalsOpen`, `a9q1-PortalsClue`, `BanditAfterFightTalk`
  (and, by the presets, `a9q1-KilledDoedre/Maligaro/Shavronne` and `AfterBanditFight`) as session-only. The generic
  NPCTalk QF4 would save `a9q1-PortalsOpen`, which keeps QS Order 0 from ever matching. The server needs
  session (unsaved) quest flags, or at least must clear these on leaving the area.
- `a9q1-KilledBoss` has no client setter; `AfterBanditFight` (QF 55, also the act 2 bandit flag and cleared by Piety's
  rows 628–633) is reused here as the "fight over" flag.
- Options `_take_ingredients_` / `_take_ingredient1_` / `_take_ingredient2_` start with `_`: probably not shown as
  normal dialogue options (auto hand-in); the driver may need the option index (unverified).
- Basilisk Acid's drop is not in the tables (wiki only); SinQuarry, SinCore and Lilly are out-of-town NPCs; the membrane
  transition is a quest-gated tile object (AreaTransition + Transitionable, the gate-opening problem of
  [12](../../protocol/12-quests.md) §6).
- Lilly's `AreaTransitionTo` needs server code (fallback for the driver: `goto 2_10_town via command`, which skips
  nothing flag-wise: `Visited-10_town` is set by the entry).
