# Harbinger

The opt-in targets the Phrecia Occultist graph for client 3.29.3b.
Ordinary Occultist does not match it; admission retains the installed-graph check.
Publication depends on the accepted Whisperer prerequisite in #333.

## Native contracts and findings

The three passive-granted summon catalog rows have empty cost amounts and a six-second cooldown.
Their normal admission is zero-cost; a positive payment claim would be incorrect.
Focus instead requires a current paid channel receipt before a new companion cast.
Ending the channel does not remove an already received Focus effect.

Native duration fields carry remaining BASE seconds, while recipient stats control the elapsed rate.
Those stats must reach the client before the corresponding buff add.
Time publishes an eight-second BASE duration.
The category-one rate source changes its clock; the category-four Diamond Flask remains a six-second
negative control and consumes its normal charges.
Pause consumes no remaining BASE budget; changing a source settles the old rate first.

Level-one Temporal Chains projects target action speed -15 and other-effect elapsed rate -25.
Blasphemy's less-effect term truncates these to -11/-18; reflected player effect truncates to -7/-12.
Timeclasp suppression leaves the original timed curse running, so unequipping restores only its remainder.
Maintained Blasphemy preserves its instance beyond the timed curse's five-second duration.
The curse's own duration excludes its other-effects rate.

Successful travel consumes the pending request before the source connection closes.
Roster preservation therefore uses the accepted Auth handoff, independently of pending intent.
The destination builds stats/pools while initial Life is zero, before restoring vitals.
That pre-object state must not acquire a roster lease or be treated as an actual death.
Provider replacement retires the optional area; Game replacement also requires fresh session ownership.
Labyrinth trap degeneration shares received Focus and fractional non-chaos ES bypass with the other DoT sources.
The scheduled resource writer retains lethal trap death and refuses Mana recovery after that death.

## Model boundaries

The unavailable official AIS is represented by metadata order, initial cooldown, a 700 ms phase,
per-source cooldown suspension and a twelve-cell follow policy.
Companion/curse observations use the existing 100 ms minion schedule.
Party propagation and strongest-of-multiple-casters curse arbitration remain outside this model.

Bounded client observations cover the three actors, paid Focus, direct/aura/reflected curses,
Time pause/rate changes, the flask negative control, successful travel, reload and death removal.
They do not establish whole-class or official-server parity.
The full recipient category matrix, Hexproof/immunity and unaffordable-aura controls, raw action/chill
cadence, failed-transfer/race interleavings and full Ruthless coverage remain incomplete.
Exact revisions, checks and evidence are recorded in [PR #339](https://github.com/LocalExile/LocalExile/pull/339).
